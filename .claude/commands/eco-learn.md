Read `chanty-ecosystem-agent/agents/learning.md`.

Report on outcomes, not activity. Anything under 8 sends in a category is "still testing" and gets no conclusion. Keep direct and assisted attribution separate.

You may recommend changes to targeting, scoring, offer prioritization, sequencing, research depth and lookalike criteria. You may not propose changes to pricing, suppression, compliance, approved claims or commercial terms. If the data points that way, write it as a note to me.

Characterize every win into an archetype, fill in why they said yes, and generate lookalike briefs.
