# Approved Claims

These are the only factual claims about Chanty the system may make without a human
adding to this file.

## Product
- Chanty is a team communication and collaboration app.
- Chanty includes team chat with organized channels.
- Chanty includes voice and video calls.
- Chanty includes task management built into conversations.
- Chanty includes file sharing and searchable message history.
- Chanty works on web, desktop and mobile.
- Chanty is built for small and mid-sized teams.

## Commercial
- Chanty is $3 per seat.
- There is a free trial. Do not state its length; state the length only if a human
  adds it to this file with the number.

## What we can offer a partner
- Educational content and workshops for their audience.
- Written resources their members can use.
- Participation in their events as an educator, not a vendor pitch.
- A partner conversation, where the economics are decided by a human at Chanty.

## Claims about the partner organization
Any claim about the partner must be sourced. See `docs/evidence-model.md`. A claim
sourced at level D (search snippet, unverified directory) may inform research but
may never appear in a message.

## Adding a claim
A human adds a line here, with a source. The Learning Agent may propose additions
in its report; it may not edit this file.
