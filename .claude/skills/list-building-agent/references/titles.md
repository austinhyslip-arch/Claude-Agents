# Title matching

Target roles are the person who runs the practice day-to-day, not clinical staff and not
corporate/system-level roles.

## Clean matches (Title Match = Matched)

- Office Manager / Front Office Manager
- Practice Manager / Practice Administrator / Practice Director
- Clinic Manager / Clinic Administrator / Clinic Director
- Owner / Practice Owner / Founder (small independent practices are often owner-operated —
  a solo or small-group owner counts even without a separate "manager" title)
- Managing Partner
- Office Administrator / Business Manager / Business Administrator
- Operations Manager (only at a single small practice — see exclusions below)
- General Manager (only at a single small practice — see exclusions below)

## Fuzzy but acceptable — match with a note

Titles that combine a qualifying word with something else, e.g. "Office Manager / Billing
Coordinator," "Practice Manager & RN." Include, `Title Match` = `Matched`, keep the full
title as found.

## Flag rather than drop (Title Match = Flagged — unclear title)

- Titles that could be the decision-maker but don't cleanly match: "Administrator" alone
  (no "office/practice/clinic" qualifier), "Director of Operations," "COO" at a practice
  small enough that the title might just mean the same thing as office manager, "Practice
  Coordinator."
- A contact search that returns no title at all but the person is clearly senior (e.g.
  found on the "leadership" or "our team" page without a listed role).

Never guess a flagged title into a clean match, and never drop it — put it in the sheet
with the flag so Austin makes the call.

## Exclude outright (do not add as a contact)

Clinical-only titles with no management signal (RN, MA, front desk/receptionist without a
"manager" qualifier), and corporate/system-level titles (Regional Manager, VP of
Operations, Director of Practice Management at a chain HQ) — those belong to the excluded
company anyway if the independence check is working, but exclude the title even if it
somehow shows up on an independent practice's page, since it's not the buyer this agent is
built to find.

## Multiple qualifying contacts at one practice

Small practices sometimes have both an owner and a separate office manager. Include both as
separate rows, linked to the same company. Don't try to guess which one is "more" the
decision-maker — that's Austin's call during review.
