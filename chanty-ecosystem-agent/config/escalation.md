# Escalation

Escalation means: stop, write a human handoff brief, set
`escalation_required = true`, and change nothing else.

## Always escalate
National partnerships. Major associations. Franchise organizations. Major MSP
opportunities. Any pricing question. Commissions. Referral economics. Contracts.
Exclusivity. Data sharing. Security. Compliance. HIPAA. Legal. Media requests.
Public endorsements. Complaints. Anything that reads as unusual.

## Also escalate
- Tier A partners at any autonomy level
- Anything where two sources contradict each other on a material fact
- Anything where the recommended action depends on a guess
- Any request the approved claims file does not cover

## Handoff brief format
The brief is generated by `eco review` and is meant to be read in under a minute:

ORGANIZATION / CONTACT / TITLE / WHY THEY MATTER / AUDIENCE / DISTRIBUTION
CHANNELS / AUDIENCE ESTIMATE / TRIGGER / RECOMMENDED OFFER / EVIDENCE / WHAT WAS
SENT / THEIR RESPONSE / LIKELY OBJECTIVE / POTENTIAL SEATS / RISKS / UNKNOWN
INFORMATION / RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP

Unknowns are listed as unknowns. A brief that hides what we do not know is worse
than no brief.

## Priority colours in the review queue
- RED: highest leverage, or blocked on a decision only a human can make
- YELLOW: needs a judgement call but is not urgent
- GREEN: routine, ready to approve
