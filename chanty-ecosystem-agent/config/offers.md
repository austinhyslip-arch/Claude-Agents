# Approved Offer Library

Every offer below is pre-approved as a *thing we can talk about*. None of them
carry commercial terms beyond $3/seat.

## 1. Educational workshop
For organizations that already run webinars, workshops, lunch-and-learns or
member education.

Titles that have been approved for use:
- How to Build a Communication System for a Growing Team
- Stop Running Your Business Through Email and Text
- The Small Business Communication Stack
- Practical AI for Team Communication
- How to Reduce Communication Chaos as Your Team Grows

## 2. Member resource
For organizations that publish, curate or distribute member resources.

- Small Business Team Communication Playbook
- Communication Policy Template
- Team Channel Structure Guide
- Notification Guidelines
- Team Onboarding Checklist
- AI Communication Guidelines

## 3. Member benefit
Chanty at standard pricing, $3/seat. This is listed as a benefit because access
and support are the benefit. It is not a discount and must never be described as
one. Do not write "special rate", "member rate", "exclusive pricing", or anything
in that family.

## 4. Partner / referral program
Relevant to MSPs, IT consultants, business consultants, coaches, technology
advisors. The agent may say a partner conversation makes sense. The agent may not
state terms, percentages, or structures. Escalate.

## 5. Co-branded content
Webinar, guide, report, checklist, survey, educational series.

## 6. Event participation
Speaker, panel, workshop, office hours, educational session.

## 7. Discovery conversation
When none of the above clearly fits, the honest move is a short conversation about
whether there is a fit at all. Do not force an offer onto an organization that has
no matching distribution mechanism.

## Selection logic

| Organization does this | Offer |
|---|---|
| Runs webinars or workshops | Educational workshop |
| Has a formal member benefits program | Member benefit |
| Refers technology or vendors to clients | Partner / referral |
| Publishes educational content | Member resource or co-branded content |
| Runs events | Event participation |
| None of the above is verified | Discovery conversation |
