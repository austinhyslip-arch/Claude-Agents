# Email format

One email per run, to **austinhyslip@gmail.com**. Plain text or light HTML — it needs to be
readable on a phone in thirty seconds.

## Subject lines

- Weekly: `[Weekly] Competitive Digest — Aug 24, 2026`
- On-demand: `[On-Demand] Competitive Check — last 24 hours`
- Discovery month: append ` + Discovery` to either.
- Big Stuff present: append ` — N flags` so it is visible before opening.

Example: `[Weekly] Competitive Digest — Aug 24, 2026 + Discovery — 2 flags`

## Body structure

```
BIG STUFF
(Only if flags scored 4+. Omit the whole section when there are none — do not
write "no big stuff" at the top; note it in one line at the end of the email
instead.)

• <Competitor> — <what happened, one sentence>. <Why it matters to Chanty, one
  sentence.> [source link, date]

—

PRICING COMPLAINTS
Grouped by competitor, only competitors with something to report.

<Competitor>
• <The complaint, one or two sentences.> [G2 / Capterra / Reddit, date, link]

FEATURE COMPLAINTS
Same shape.

<Competitor>
• <The complaint, one or two sentences.> [source, date, link]

CHANTY MENTIONS
• <What was said, where, one or two sentences.> [source, date, link]
(or: "No Chanty mentions this window.")

DISCOVERY — NEW TIER 3 (monthly runs only)
• <Name> — surfaced in <source A> and <source B>. <One line on what it is.>
• Dormant: <names moved to dormant this pass, if any>

—
Window: <start> to <end> · Sources: G2, Capterra, Reddit, Hacker News, competitor
changelogs and pricing pages · No Big Stuff flags this window. (last line only when
the Big Stuff section was omitted)
```

## Writing rules

- Big Stuff first, always. Never open with review complaints when a flag exists.
- One or two sentences per item. If it needs three, it is two items or it is a link.
- Lead each complaint line with the substance, not the source: "Seat minimum of 3 forces
  small teams to overpay" beats "A G2 reviewer said that...".
- Every line ends with source and date. No bare assertions.
- Competitors with nothing to report are omitted, not listed as empty.
- No preamble, no sign-off, no "hope this helps". The email starts with the first heading.
- Say when a window was thin: "Quiet week — 4 items across all sources" is useful
  information, and better than padding the digest to look busy.
